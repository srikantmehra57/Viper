# Viper product website

## Product facts
A native SwiftUI Mac maintenance app, requiring macOS 14 or later. It scans selected folders for large files, removes applications and their associated data after review, finds junk and leftovers, checks updates, offers privacy settings shortcuts, and discovers/install apps using existing package managers. Review precedes changes. Cleanup moves files to Trash. No daemon, login item, scheduled task, telemetry, or background polling. No invented customer metrics, testimonials, pricing, performance gains, or security guarantees.

## Creative direction
An editorial product experience with spacious graphite surfaces, mint highlights, and localized lavender/teal light. Large confident typography, hairline dividers, asymmetric composition, and rich but disciplined motion. Central spatial metaphor: the noise of everyday use resolves into an ordered, breathing field around Viper. Use a live interactive Three.js object in the hero with a fallback and reduced-motion behavior. Never put functionality behind animation. Do not claim unprecedented originality or awards.

## Narrative
1. Navigation: Viper, Explore, The experience, Philosophy, Get Viper.
2. Hero: Your Mac. / Room to breathe. Clear supporting description and working product/source action.
3. Interactive feature stage for Storage, Uninstaller, Junk & Leftovers, Updates, and Discover & Install, using actual capabilities and clearly identified illustrative data where needed.
4. A deliberate three-step rhythm: Take a look. Make the call. Move on.
5. Quiet by nature: only runs when asked, review before removal, no background agent.
6. Final acquisition section with honest distribution details and macOS requirement.
